CAMBRIDGE PATHWAY

From Foundation to Advanced University Entrance

A continuous, step-by-step secondary curriculum ensuring mastery at every milestone.

08

Grade 8

Core level introduction to the IGCSE framework. Learners build analytical thinking, subject vocabulary, and essential scientific methodology.

09–10

IGCSE (Grades 9–10)

Two-year comprehensive IGCSE syllabus culminating in official external Cambridge International examinations written at the end of Grade 10.

11–12

AS / A Level

Senior pre-university certificates written at the end of Grade 12 (and selected A Levels), providing globally recognised matriculation endorsements.

EVALUATION FRAMEWORK

Continuous Assessment & Formal Cambridge Examinations

Student evaluation is ongoing through regular tests and assignments. Formal test weeks are scheduled during the first and third terms, with full-scale examinations at the end of the second and fourth terms. A mock final examination is held in September for learners writing final Cambridge examinations in October/November.

Ridgefield Academy is a registered International Cambridge School and examination centre (School number ZA338).
