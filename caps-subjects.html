CHOICE PROCESS

Make subject decisions in the right order.

A good subject plan starts with requirements, then considers learner performance and future direction.

01

Confirm requirements

Check the current Ridgefield offering and any compulsory grade requirements.

02

Review evidence

Use current marks, teacher feedback and learner strengths rather than preference alone.

03

Plan progression

Consider intended post-school study and keep appropriate options open where possible.
