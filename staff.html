PERSONALISED MENTORSHIP

Subject specialists committed to individual growth.

At Ridgefield Academy, classes are kept deliberately small (no more than 15 students) to ensure every learner receives focused instruction and personal attention. Our educators are subject specialists experienced in both the international Cambridge curriculum and South African national curriculum.

Explore our educators below, learn about their academic background, and view the specific subject curricula they lead.
