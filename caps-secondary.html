CAPS AT RIDGEFIELD

Structured learning with close educator monitoring.

The CAPS Secondary pathway is organised around clear academic progression, consistent term milestones, and individual support. We guide learners through the crucial adolescent years with structured classroom learning and regular performance feedback.

Our educators teach across Mathematics, Sciences, Languages, Humanities, and Commerce, creating a disciplined yet supportive school environment where every learner can flourish.

GRADE JOURNEY

Plan the secondary years as one connected pathway.

Our curriculum structure is divided into three purposeful planning stages to support learners as their academic needs evolve toward Grade 12.

01 Senior Phase · Grades 8–9

Grades 8–9 Transition

Establish consistent homework habits, strengthen foundational mathematics and languages, and monitor aptitude ahead of senior subject choices.

  • Secondary school transition & routine building
  • Quarterly assessment rhythm & feedback
  • Subject strength and career aptitude guidance
02 FET Phase · Grade 10

Grade 10 Subject Selection

Enter the formal FET phase with chosen subject streams aligned to tertiary entry and career paths, supported by targeted subject guidance.

  • Streamlined subject specialization
  • SBA baseline tracking from Term 1
  • Academic support clinics for challenging areas
03 FET Phase · Grades 11–12

Grades 11–12 Matric Preparation

Intensive exam preparation, preliminary trial examinations, and university degree endorsement focus leading to the National Senior Certificate.

  • Past paper workshops and exam methodology
  • Formal SBA portfolio completion
  • University entrance endorsement guidance

ACADEMIC RIGOUR & MONITORING

Focused teaching for National Senior Certificate success.

Success in the South African national curriculum requires discipline, structured revision, and continuous SBA assessment. Ridgefield ensures every learner stays on track with regular diagnostic testing and individual academic intervention.

Continuous Assessment Term-by-term SBA portfolio building ensuring consistent exam readiness.
Small Class Attention Educators provide immediate help and active feedback in every lesson.
Matric Endorsement Focus Targeting Bachelor Degree pass requirements for tertiary entry.
Academic Clinics Structured extra-help sessions for Mathematics and Sciences.

PARENT DECISION GUIDE

Is CAPS Secondary the right fit for your learner?

Three helpful considerations to assess whether the South African national curriculum route matches your goals.

South African Universities

Local Tertiary Alignment

Direct alignment with the National Senior Certificate (NSC) points matrix required for admission to all South African universities and colleges.

Continuous SBA

Cumulative Term Progress

Best for learners who benefit from a structured, continuous School-Based Assessment model where term marks directly build toward final results.

Disciplined Routine

Individual Attention

A smaller classroom setting ensures learners who might be lost in oversized schools receive direct daily guidance, feedback, and accountability.
