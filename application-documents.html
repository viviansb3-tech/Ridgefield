Documents to Prepare

Please gather the following documents in advance of your admissions interview or registration appointment:

  • Latest School Report: The most recent academic report card or official statement of results (Grades 2–12).
  • Learner Identification: Copy of the learner's certified birth certificate or national identity document.
  • Proof of Residential Address: Recent utility bill or municipal statement (less than 3 months old).
  • Clinic / Immunisation Card (Primary): Required for Primary learners (Grades 1–7) during admissions verification.
  • Transfer or Academic Records: Official transfer letter, clearance certificate, or Cambridge examination history where applicable.

Submit the public application online first. If supporting records are required, Ridgefield admissions will advise how they should be presented during your personal consultation.

Privacy note: do not send sensitive learner documents through an unapproved public form or channel.

How Your Documentation is Processed

A simple, transparent pathway from online enrolment to verified admission.

01

Online Application

Complete the online admissions form with learner details, parental contact information, and chosen curriculum pathway without uploading any files.

02

Admissions Review

Our academic coordinators review grade availability, curriculum prerequisites, and contact you directly with an official reference number.

03

Personal Consultation

Present certified physical copies during your campus visit or interview, where placement is confirmed and enrolment is finalized.
