FLEXIBLE EXCELLENCE

Technology-supported Cambridge learning from home.

Ridgefield’s published distance-learning model combines online access, learning materials, scheduled teaching, and structured assessment guidance for non-local learners, homeschoolers, and aspiring scholars across Southern Africa.

Learners benefit from curriculum-aligned syllabus timelines, recorded video explanations, interactive teacher consultations, and rigorous preparation for external Cambridge examinations.

REMOTE ARCHITECTURE

Three Pillars of Independent Study

How our remote framework ensures identical academic standards to on-campus tuition.

01

Technology & Access

Requirements include a dedicated computer or laptop, microphone, webcam, reliable internet connection, sufficient data bandwidth, and a document printer/scanner for assignments.

02

Continuous Assessment

Experienced subject educators evaluate distance learners against the same stringent Cambridge International rubrics applied to our on-campus cohort.

03

Official Exam Centre

Final Cambridge IGCSE and AS/A Level written and practical sessions are sat under verified Cambridge conditions at Ridgefield's Wilcock Road examination centre (Centre ZA338) or approved Cambridge examination venues.

Distance Learning Tuition & Exam Registrations

Historical baseline fees list R500 per subject per month (payable over 12 months), excluding external Cambridge examination board candidate and courier levies. Contact our admissions bursar to verify the current 2026 fee schedule and registration deadlines.
