CAMBRIDGE CURRICULUM

Ridgefield Academy offers the CAPS National Curriculum in addition to the Cambridge Syllabus. Our National School is registered with the Department of Education (EMIS 445802259) as both a secondary and primary school.

We have accepted our first primary school learners in 2023, and our primary school has been growing steadily since then. Please contact our admin office for further information.
