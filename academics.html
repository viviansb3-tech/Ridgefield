CAMBRIDGE EXAMINATIONS

An internationally recognised qualification benchmark.

The Cambridge pathway provides an internationally recognised secondary-school route with direct university progression options in South Africa and overseas.

Cambridge IGCSE and AS Level curricula build conceptual depth, analytical thinking, and independent inquiry. Learners complete external examinations evaluated to rigorous international standards.

TUITION & MENTORSHIP

Focused teaching in small class environments.

Ridgefield places emphasis on focused teaching, small-class support and individual academic attention across its programmes. Teachers identify gaps early, encourage class participation, and offer structured academic support clinics.

ACADEMIC REGISTRATION

Cambridge alongside CAPS National Curriculum.

Ridgefield Academy offers the CAPS National Curriculum in addition to the Cambridge Syllabus. The National School is registered with the Department of Education (EMIS 445802259) as both a secondary and primary school.

Families can consult our academic advisors to determine which curriculum best suits their learner's tertiary study intentions and career goals.
