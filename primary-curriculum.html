DEVELOPMENTAL JOURNEY

Learning expectations grow with the learner.

The Primary pathway is planned in stages so families can see how support, independence and academic responsibility evolve from Grade 1 to Grade 7.

01

Grades 1–3

Build the core habits and skills that make later learning possible.

  • Reading and language foundations
  • Early numeracy
  • Classroom routines
  • Confidence and participation
02

Grades 4–6

Increase independence, depth and responsibility across a broader learning programme.

  • Independent reading
  • Extended written work
  • Mathematical reasoning
  • Projects and research habits
03

Grade 7

Prepare for the organisation, academic expectations and responsibility of secondary school.

  • Study routines
  • Task planning
  • Transition guidance
  • Secondary-pathway awareness

PROGRESS EVIDENCE

Look at the whole pattern of development.

Primary progress includes academic outcomes as well as the routines and independence that support future learning.

01

Literacy

Reading, comprehension, vocabulary and written communication.

02

Numeracy

Number confidence, reasoning and mathematical fluency.

03

Learning habits

Organisation, participation, persistence and task completion.

04

Parent reporting

Clear feedback about academic progress and areas requiring reinforcement.

THE PRIMARY WEEK

A structured and balanced learning timetable.

The exact daily schedule is set by Ridgefield. This framework explains the kinds of learning experiences families should expect across a Primary week.

Literacy & communication

Reading, vocabulary, written expression and age-appropriate language development.

Numeracy & reasoning

Mathematical fluency, problem-solving and guided practice appropriate to the grade.

Broader learning

Natural, social, creative and practical learning that expands knowledge and participation.

Reinforcement & feedback

Teacher-guided consolidation, correction and support where a learner needs more practice.

Exact school hours, subject periods, break times and daily timetables should be confirmed directly with Ridgefield for the learner's grade.
