BEFORE YOU APPLY

Know what is confirmed and what should still be checked.

Operational details such as the exact timetable, current subject combination, fees and staff assignments can change. This page separates stable pathway information from details families should confirm with Ridgefield.

Which grades are included in CAPS Secondary?

The pathway is presented for Grades 8–12.

Where is CAPS Secondary based?

The pathway is presented at Ridgefield's Maartens Street campus, 22 Maartens Street, Bloemfontein.

Which subjects can my learner take?

The website provides subject-planning areas based on the current Ridgefield staff directory. The exact grade-specific subject set must be confirmed with the school before enrolment or subject selection.

How does assessment work?

The pathway is planned around regular teaching, tasks, tests, progress review and response. Exact assessment dates, weightings and examination schedules remain school-controlled and should be confirmed for the learner's grade.

What does a normal school week look like?

Families should expect scheduled subject teaching, guided practice, independent work, assessment preparation and feedback. Ridgefield's current official timetable controls exact periods, start times and day-to-day scheduling.

How are parents kept informed?

The pathway is structured around academic records, attendance and progress reporting. Families should confirm the school's current reporting calendar and communication process.

Do I upload identity documents with the online application?

No. The public application does not accept sensitive learner-document uploads. Prepare the requested documents for the admissions consultation and use only an approved secure channel if the school later requests copies.

Can we visit before applying?

The application questionnaire allows families to indicate that they would like a school visit. You can also contact Ridgefield directly before submitting an application.

How do we confirm fees and availability?

Use the current Fees page and contact Ridgefield for the latest grade availability and operational details. Unconfirmed historical values should not be treated as current.
