COMMON PREPARATION

Have the learner's current academic information ready.

Ridgefield's published admissions information states that applicants need information from their previous school. The exact requirements can differ by pathway and grade, so confirm the current list with the Academy.

  • Latest available school report or statement of results
  • Learner identity documentation prepared for the admissions consultation
  • Proof of address prepared where requested
  • Transfer or programme-specific records where applicable
  • Questions about grade placement, subjects or learner support

Privacy note: sensitive learner documents are not uploaded through the public application form. Prepare them for the admissions consultation and use only an approved secure channel if Ridgefield later requests copies.

PATHWAY-SPECIFIC NOTES

What the current website supports.

These notes distinguish published information from items that still need confirmation for the learner's specific application.

CAMBRIDGE

Assessment may form part of entry.

Ridgefield's published information states that Cambridge applicants may have a personal interview and, where applicable, an entrance assessment in one or more subjects.

CAPS

Confirm grade and current subject offering.

Grade placement is based on previous school reports and an admissions consultation. Contact Ridgefield for subject choice guidance and placement review.

PRIMARY

Confirm grade placement and current programme.

Families should discuss the learner's current grade, school history and intended start with Ridgefield before finalising the application.
