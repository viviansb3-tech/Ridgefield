STUDENT WELLBEING & COMMUNITY · RIDGEFIELD ACADEMY

Education is more than good teaching. It is life in community.

We believe that a student's wellbeing is nurtured through genuine encouragement, small-group camaraderie, sports, creative exploration, and shared traditions in a safe and supportive school home.

COMMUNITY ATMOSPHERE

A place where every learner belongs.

Student life at Ridgefield brings together classroom learning, personal educator support, shared school experiences, and opportunities to build lasting confidence.

Because classes are kept intentionally small, no learner is overlooked. Friendships cross grades naturally, and learners learn to encourage and support each other's progress.

ANNUAL TRADITIONS

Celebrating milestones and character growth.

Each academic year culminates in celebrations that recognise both academic effort and character growth. From our Primary awards to Cambridge and CAPS Matric valedictory events, Ridgefield honours the unique journey of every learner.

RECREATION & HEALTH

Spacious grounds for activity, sport and friendship.

Both our Noordhoek and Maartens Street campuses offer protected outdoor recreation areas suitable for active break times and sports including soccer and volleyball.

We believe regular physical movement, fresh air, and unstructured social time are essential counterparts to rigorous intellectual study.
