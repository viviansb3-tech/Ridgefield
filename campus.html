PURPOSE-BUILT ENVIRONMENT

Designed for learning, practical discovery, and community life.

The official campus description places Ridgefield Academy in the northern part of Bloemfontein, on the peaceful edge of Waverley. The school comprises two adjacent properties, designated as Block A and Block B, creating a focused and secure academic precinct.

Published campus facilities include modern classrooms, administrative offices, an accredited national and Cambridge examination centre, a fully equipped science laboratory, resource library, student tuckshop and kitchen, safe room, storerooms, book shop, and expansive outdoor grounds.

✓
Accredited Examination Centre — Secure facilities for CAIE and national curriculum examinations.
✓
Fully Equipped Science Laboratory — Hands-on experimental stations for Physics and Chemistry.
✓
