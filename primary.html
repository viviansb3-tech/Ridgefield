PRIMARY AT RIDGEFIELD

Build confidence before the pressure of secondary school.

The primary school years establish a child's relationship with learning for life. At Ridgefield, we combine strong foundational academics with genuine encouragement, routine, and individual care.

Our educators focus on reading comprehension, confident numeracy, creative exploration, and positive social interaction, giving children a secure foundation to grow into self-motivated learners.

GRADE JOURNEY

Three purposeful phases of childhood academic growth.

Carefully structured across the primary years to build secure habits, deepen conceptual understanding, and ensure a confident transition to secondary school.

01 Foundation Phase · Grades 1–3

Foundation Phase

Phonics, fluent reading, arithmetic confidence, fine motor coordination, and a warm classroom routine that makes learning joyful and secure.

  • Early literacy & structured phonics routines
  • Hands-on numeracy and spatial reasoning
  • Personal development, empathy and classroom socialization
02 Intermediate Phase · Grades 4–6

Intermediate Phase

Expanding into structured subject disciplines, reading for information, multi-step mathematics, and scientific observation with gentle exam readiness.

  • Transition to subject-specific educators
  • Independent study habits and project work
  • Natural sciences and social enquiry skills
03 Senior Primary Transition · Grade 7

Senior Primary Transition

Higher-order critical thinking, structured research projects, formal examination practice, and preparation for secondary school pathways under CAPS.

  • Formal assessment and time management skills
  • Analytical writing and scientific problem-solving
  • Clear pathway alignment for Cambridge or CAPS Secondary

PARENT DECISION GUIDE

Is Ridgefield Primary the right school for your child?

Three core reasons parents choose Ridgefield Academy for their child's foundational primary education.

Never Lost in the Crowd

Small Learning Groups

Unlike massive primary schools with 35+ learners per room, our smaller classes ensure your child is known personally, heard, and supported daily.

Core Academic Rigour

Secondary Readiness
