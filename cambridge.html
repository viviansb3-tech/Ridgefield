CAMBRIDGE AT RIDGEFIELD

A proven global standard taught with individual attention.

Ridgefield Academy provides a rigorous, internationally respected British curriculum through Cambridge Assessment International Education (CAIE). Our learners develop conceptual depth, independent critical inquiry, and analytical reasoning.

Classes are strictly capped at 15 learners, ensuring every learner receives personal mentorship, regular feedback, and structured preparation for external international examinations.

CURRICULUM ROADMAP

A clear progression from lower secondary to university readiness.

The Cambridge pathway is planned as a coherent academic journey from foundation skills through to advanced university entrance qualifications.

01 Ages 13–14 · Grade 8

Pre-IGCSE Foundation

Build core mathematical fluency, scientific inquiry, analytical reading, and self-directed study routines before entering exam courses.

  • Mathematical problem-solving foundations
  • Hands-on science laboratory introduction
  • Critical English reading & structured writing
02 Ages 14–16 · Grades 9–10

Cambridge IGCSE

The world's most popular international qualification for secondary learners. External examinations benchmarked to global standards.

  • In-depth subject syllabus coverage
  • Practical science laboratory papers
  • Internationally recognised qualification certificates
03 Ages 16–18 · Grades 11–12

Cambridge AS & A Level

Advanced pre-university academic depth designed to meet university entrance requirements in South Africa, the UK, Europe, and beyond (subject to individual subject choices and faculty prerequisites).

  • Higher-tier conceptual mastery
  • USAf Matriculation Board exemption alignment
  • Intensive examination preparation & coaching

PARENT DECISION GUIDE

Is the Cambridge pathway right for your child?

Three key considerations to help you decide whether an international syllabus matches your learner's academic profile.

Global Ambition

International University Aspiration

Best for families planning higher education in the UK, Europe, the United States, Australia, or aiming for direct USAf Matriculation Board exemption in South Africa.

Analytical Thinkers

Conceptual Mastery

Ideal for learners who excel at deep inquiry, mathematical problem-solving, structured writing, and independent intellectual curiosity rather than rote memorisation.

Focused Attention

Small-Class Mentorship

Strictly capped at 15 learners, ensuring quiet, focused classrooms where educators closely track progress, identify gaps early, and provide bespoke guidance.
