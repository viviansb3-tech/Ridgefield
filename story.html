INTERNATIONAL CAMBRIDGE SCHOOL

A school built around international learning and personal mentorship.

According to Ridgefield Academy’s published history, the school was founded in 2012 in Bloemfontein to respond to demand for a world-class international education. It began with a small group of high-school learners and later expanded to larger premises.

The school is described as a registered International Cambridge School and examination centre, with School number ZA338, and is also registered with the South African Department of Education under EMIS 445802259.

TIMELINE

Milestones in Our Journey

How Ridgefield evolved to meet the growing educational needs of Free State learners.

2012

Ridgefield’s published history places the founding of the school in Bloemfontein in 2012, answering parental demand for international secondary education.

2013–2018

The school expanded through successive premises as learner numbers and the need for larger scientific and recreational facilities grew.

2020

The National Curriculum was introduced alongside the Cambridge syllabus in response to parent demand for high-standard CAPS secondary pathways.

2023

The first learners were accepted into the primary-school programme, completing the continuous Grade 1–12 educational journey.

LEARNING FROM THE HEART

Education with purpose and character.

The published school history connects the name Ridgefield with the relationship between nature and humanity and describes the motto Learning from the Heart as an emphasis on responsibility, relationships and personal development alongside academic achievement.

This philosophy underpins every aspect of student life—from small, conversational seminar classrooms to practical science laboratory workshops and mutual respect across year groups.

OUR STORY · CAMBRIDGE

Our Cambridge pathway

Ridgefield Academy offers a wide range of subjects for the Cambridge International Education Examinations. Tuition is offered from Grade 8 to Grade 12, with the IGCSE (International General Certificate of Secondary Education) external examination written at the end of Grade 10 and the AS (Advanced Subsidiary) Certificate at the end of Grade 12. Selected A Level courses are also offered.

Grades 8–12

Grade 8: Core Level / Introduction to IGCSE

Grade 9: IGCSE 1

Grade 10: IGCSE 2 + International Examination

Grade 11: AS 1

Grade 12: AS 2 + International Examination

Evaluation & examination

Student evaluation is ongoing through regular tests and assignments. Formal test weeks are scheduled during the first and third terms, with full-scale examinations at the end of the second and fourth terms. A mock final examination is held in September for learners writing final Cambridge examinations in October/November.

Subjects available

Ridgefield's published Cambridge subject offering includes English (First Language), Afrikaans (Second Language), French (Second Language), Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Geography, Business Studies, Accounting, Computer Science and History, with IGCSE and/or AS availability depending on the subject.

Hands-on tuition

Classes are kept small, with no more than 15 students. Ridgefield places strong emphasis on individual attention and student participation and provides practical science tuition through its equipped laboratory, including practical training and guidance in Physics and Chemistry.

Recognition

The Cambridge syllabus is recognised by universities worldwide. University and faculty entry requirements vary, so learners are encouraged to check the exact Cambridge subject and level requirements for their intended university and faculty.

Source: Ridgefield Academy — Cambridge Curriculum & Syllabus · Cambridge International
