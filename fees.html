CURRENT FEE SCHEDULE

Fees vary by academic pathway and grade level.

Cambridge International, CAPS Secondary, and Primary fee structures are individually tailored to class sizes, practical laboratory sessions, and curriculum materials. Rates can adjust between academic cycles.

To avoid relying on outdated amounts, please request the official current schedule before enrolling or executing payments.

When enquiring about fees:

Please specify the learner's intended programme (Cambridge, CAPS Secondary, or Primary) and grade level so our finance office can provide the precise fee schedule, deposit requirements, and payment schedule.

FINANCIAL POLICIES

Payment terms and payment schedule options.

Ridgefield Academy provides structured payment options to support family budgeting. Prior to payment, our administration office will confirm the applicable registration deposit, termly or monthly payment options, and official bank details.

✓
Registration & Enrolment — Confirm the applicable registration fee upon acceptance to secure your learner's seat.
✓
Payment Schedule Options — Annual, termly, and monthly instalment options available upon request.
✓
Direct EFT Verification — Always verify banking details directly with the official Ridgefield office before transferring funds.
