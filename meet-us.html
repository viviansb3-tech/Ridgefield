OUR HERITAGE & VISION

Academic excellence grounded in personal attention.

Ridgefield Academy was established to provide an educational home where learners receive rigorous instruction without becoming lost in oversized classrooms.

Our approach combines proven international and national syllabi with close mentorship, strong family communication, and an environment that celebrates individual learner potential.

TWO LEARNING ENVIRONMENTS

A place to learn, explore and belong.

Cambridge Campus: 25 Wilcock Road, Noordhoek.
Dedicated to international IGCSE and AS Level secondary education with fully equipped physics, chemistry, and biology laboratories.

National Curriculum Campus: 22 Maartens Street.
Home to our CAPS Secondary (Grades 8–12) and Primary School (Grades 1–7) with spacious grounds, sports fields, and library.

OUR SYLLABUS

Cambridge alongside CAPS National Curriculum.

Ridgefield Academy offers both the Cambridge Assessment International Education curriculum and the South African CAPS National Curriculum. The National School is registered with the Department of Education (EMIS 445802259) as both a secondary and primary school.
